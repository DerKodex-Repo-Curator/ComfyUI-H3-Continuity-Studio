# Trailer

Make the video play like a trailer for a longer story: it promises the story instead of telling all of it. Follow [the sequence](references/sequence.md) in order. It works in any medium.

## Style

- Short, purposeful shots, each showing one idea.
- The pace rises across the video: long, quiet shots at the start, quick cuts in the montage, then one held shot before the title.
- Hard cuts, with a cut to black between the main parts.
- On-screen text is short, in a bold, plain type, centered, and stays long enough to read.
- Sound: a low drone that builds, impacts on the hard cuts, a beat of silence before the title card and one last hit on the sting.

## Rules

- Never resolve the story: the trailer ends on a question.
- Show the main character's face early and clearly, so the audience knows whose story it is.
- Keep the world consistent: the same places, light and palette from the cold open to the sting.
- The title card shows the title from the idea; when the idea has none, make up a short one.
