# The trailer's sequence

1. **Cold open.** One quiet, striking moment with no explanation: the main character in an ordinary moment that feels slightly wrong. No text and no music, only the room's sound.
2. **The world.** Wide shots of where the story happens and how life goes there. The drone begins.
3. **The inciting moment.** The event that breaks the ordinary: a hard cut, an impact, then a beat of silence.
4. **Rising montage.** Quick shots of what follows: the main character acting, the stakes growing and the world reacting. Each cut lands on the music, and the cuts get faster.
5. **Title card.** Silence, then the title on black or over one held image.
6. **Final sting.** One last short moment after the title, such as a surprise, a joke or a threat, ending on a hard cut to black.
