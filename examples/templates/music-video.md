# Music video

A music video that intercuts a performance with a small story. The song's sections give the video its shape: the performance carries the energy, and the story carries the meaning. It works in any medium.

## Sequence

1. **Intro.** A quiet, wide image of the story's world before the performer appears. One slow move.
2. **Verse.** The story begins: one character, one place, one small wish. The performer sings between the story's moments, framed close and steady.
3. **Chorus.** The performance opens up: wider framing, more movement, brighter light. The story cuts in on the strongest beats.
4. **Verse.** The story turns: something changes or gets in the way. The performer is closer and the light is lower.
5. **Chorus.** Bigger than the first: faster cuts, the motif returns, and the story and the performance start to mirror each other.
6. **Bridge.** A change of texture: slower, a single unbroken shot or a new place. The story's turning point lands here.
7. **Final chorus.** Everything at once: the fastest cutting, the fullest light, and the story resolved in one clear image.
8. **Outro.** The energy falls away. End on a held image that answers the intro.

## Style and rules

- Cut on the beat and on sung phrases; never cut in the middle of a held note.
- Keep one recurring motif, such as an object, a gesture or a color, and bring it back in every chorus.
- The performer's look never changes: the same clothes, hair and place for the performance throughout.
- The light builds into each chorus and drops back for each verse.
- The story is told in pictures, without dialogue; the song is the only voice.
- Performance shots favor steady close-ups and slow pushes in; story shots favor natural movement and wider frames.
- Sound: the song carries the video, and story moments may add quiet ambient sound under it.
