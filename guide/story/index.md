# Story

The Story features are **optional**. The Timeline accepts native H3 prompts directly and renders without any writer model.

| Page | Use it to |
| --- | --- |
| [The H3 Story writer](writer.md) | Type an idea and have a model plan the clips and write their prompts; revise with feedback. |
| [Templates](templates.md) | Make a video resemble a style and sequence you describe in a text file. |
| [Cast and the Story bible](cast.md) | Keep characters, places and props consistent, with their looks over time, voice clips and pinned moments. |

!!! info "Privacy"
    An external language-model endpoint receives your story text and, when **Send pictures to the writer** is on, your reference pictures. Check the provider's privacy and pricing terms. Models and provider services are separate from this node pack. A local endpoint (such as LM Studio) keeps everything on your machine.
